# Brand positioning

## Name

**Rumbelo** — from Spanish *rumbo*, “course / heading.”  
Chosen because it means **direction**, which is what the product does.

`.com` and `.nl` were verified free at choosing (verify with **whois**, never DNS alone). Before further commitment: BOIP (Benelux) and EUIPO trademark search, classes 36 and 42.

---

## North star

**Intention before chaos.**  
Every euro gets a job. Calm overview. Direction — for people who are doing well, and for people who are ready to.

---

## Voice

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Calm, clear, direct | Shame, panic, “you’re bad with money” |
| Intention, direction, clarity | Luxury flex, crypto hype, neon fintech |
| Information + one next move | Alarm theatre without a CTA |
| Dutch-first warmth, English clarity | Jargon, spreadsheet energy |

**Tone:** modern, trustworthy, elegant — calm, not cold. Soft ambition, not hustle.

## Visual direction (product tokens)

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Accent | `#0F766E` deep teal | Growth, control, calm |
| Ink | `#0E1116` | Primary text |
| Surfaces | `#EDEFF3` / `#F5F7F9` | Soft cool greys |
| Display font | Bricolage-style | Headlines |
| Body | Clean humanist sans | UI copy |
| Mono | Plex Mono | Labels, rituals, meta |

**Avoid:** purple/indigo as brand identity, neon, crypto gold, loud bank-blue gradients.

**Logo preference:** combination mark (text + quiet icon) + wordmark + lettermark **R**.  
Metaphors: jars/vessels, direction, quiet geometric glyph. **Not** pigs, coins, €/$, charts.

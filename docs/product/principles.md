# Product principles

These must not be violated. If a feature fights a principle, the feature loses.

---

### 1. Eerst verdelen, dan uitgeven
Money gets a job **before** it gets spent.  
Split first. Spend second.

### 2. Tien minuten per week
The weekly ritual beats daily worry.  
The product should reward a short, repeating practice — not constant vigilance.

### 3. Energie draagt geld
A tired head spends; a rested head decides.  
Energy is not a lifestyle extra — it is the floor under financial decisions.

### 4. Informatie, nooit schaamte
An over-the-line jar is a **signal**, not a verdict.  
Every warning carries the one move that fixes it.  
No alarm theatre. No moralizing.

---

## How principle 4 shows up in the product

- The Coach always ships a CTA (one clear next move).
- Overspending renders as information, not panic.
- Weak correlations stay hidden until there is enough data — six noisy points are not “insight.”
